Problem Statement:

Many individuals want to learn new practical or creative skills, but paid courses, bootcamps, and private tutoring are often too expensive, rigid, or impersonal. At the same time, countless people possess valuable expertise they are eager to share, yet they lack a structured, reliable space to connect with peers who hold complementary skills. Without a dedicated system to match complementary learning needs, collaborative peer-to-peer knowledge sharing remains fragmented and difficult to coordinate. Skillswap solves this by providing a community-driven platform where users can freely trade skills-teaching what they know in exchange for learning what they need. By leveraging smart skill-tag matching, dedicated “teach and learn” user profiles, a seamless swap request flow, and a community review system, skillswap removes financial barriers and creates a trusted, collaborative environment for mutual learning.
